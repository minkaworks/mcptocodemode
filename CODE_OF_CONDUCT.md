# Code of conduct

People who take part in issues, pull requests, and discussions on minkaworks repositories:

- Talk about the work.
- Keep other people's credentials, private messages, and unreleased personal data out of the thread.
- Leave harassment, threats, and sexual content out of the project.

A maintainer may edit a comment, lock a thread, or block an account that breaks these rules. They name the rule in the thread, unless saying it would spread a private report.

## Reporting

This project does not publish a separate mailbox. [minkaworks.com](https://www.minkaworks.com/en#contact) is the company page and does not list an address.

To report conduct in private, open a [draft security advisory](https://github.com/minkaworks/mcptocodemode/security/advisories/new) and start the title with `Conduct:`. Maintainers read that queue. Public issues are for the work.
